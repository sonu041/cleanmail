# CleanMail User Guide & Features ✉️

Welcome to **CleanMail**! CleanMail is designed to give you a calm, distraction-free, and blazing-fast email experience. It strips out heavy tracking pixels, bloated formatting, and clutter, leaving you with clean, readable text and effortless inbox triage.

This guide walks you through every feature available in the application, how to use it, and how to navigate between screens.

## 1. The Inbox

The Inbox is your central command feed where messages from all your connected accounts appear chronologically.

### Reading List & Clean Typography
- Emails are presented with clean, distraction-free typography.
- Senders, subjects, preview snippets, and short timestamps are visible at a glance.
- An account color tag (such as blue or purple) tells you which mailbox received the message.

### Swipe to Delete
- **Swipe Left**: Swiping any email tile from right to left deletes it.
- Swiping right is disabled to prevent accidental actions.

### Undo Protection
- Whenever you delete an email, a toast message appears at the bottom for **5 seconds** confirming the deletion.
- Tap **Undo** to immediately bring the email back to your inbox.
- If you don't tap Undo, the message automatically dismisses after 5 seconds.

### Unread Indicator Dot
- Unread emails feature a distinct **green dot** next to the sender's name.
- Once you tap an email to read it, the green dot disappears.

### Email Locking & Protection
Need to protect an important message like a tax receipt, flight ticket, or password reset?
- **Visual Lock Badge**: Locked emails prominently display a golden **Locked** tag with a lock icon.
- **Accidental Deletion Blocked**: Swiping left to delete a locked email is prevented. A notification reminds you: *"This email is locked. Unlock it first to delete."*
- **Protected in Bulk Actions**: When deleting multiple emails or sweeping an entire sender, locked emails are automatically skipped and preserved safely.

### Category Tags & Sender Preferences
Every email has an intelligent category tag (such as **Personal**, **Newsletters**, **Transactions**, or **Updates**).
- **Change or Set Preferred Tag**: Tap directly on the category chip on any email tile.
- **Set for this Sender**: Choose a category or enter a custom label name (e.g. *Family*, *Receipts*, *VIP*).
- **Auto-Lock Future Emails**: Check "Auto-lock future emails from this sender" to guarantee that incoming messages from this person are locked and protected automatically.
- **Apply to Existing**: Choose whether to apply this new rule to all existing messages from this sender in your inbox.

### Multi-Select & Batch Actions
You can select multiple emails at once to triage them together:
- **How to Activate**: **Long-press** on any email tile. You will feel a gentle haptic vibration, and checkboxes will appear.
- **Select More**: Tap any other emails to check or uncheck them.
- **Top Selection Bar**:
  - **Cancel (X)**: Exits selection mode.
  - **Counter**: Shows how many emails are currently selected.
  - **Select All / Deselect All**: Tap the checklist icon to select every email in your current view in one tap.
  - **Lock / Unlock Selected**: Toggle protection for all selected emails simultaneously.
  - **Delete Selected**: Deletes all selected emails (with instant Undo protection). If any locked emails are included, CleanMail safely preserves them.

### Search
- Tap the **Search (magnifying glass)** icon in the top navigation bar.
- Type any word to instantly search sender names, email addresses, subjects, and email text. Tap **X** to clear and close search.

### Loading Older Emails
- Scroll down to the very end of your inbox list.
- Tap the **"Sync Next 20 Older Emails"** button to load the next set of older emails from your mailbox history.

## 2. Filters & Mailbox Switching

Keep your screen spacious with CleanMail's collapsible filter drawer.

### Sliding Filter Bar
- Tap the **Sliders (Tune)** icon in the top right corner of the screen.
- The filter panel slides smoothly in and out from the right.
- If an active filter is applied while the panel is closed, a small green dot reminds you that a filter is active.

### Switching Mailboxes
- Inside the filter section, you'll see chips for **All Inboxes** and each of your individual mailboxes (showing your email prefix and active email count).
- Tap an individual mailbox chip to isolate messages from that account, or tap **All Inboxes** to see everything together.
- Tap **+ Add Mailbox** to quickly connect a new account.

### Unread Only Filter
- In the filter section, tap the **Unread** button (showing how many unread emails you have).
- The inbox instantly filters to display only emails that haven't been opened yet. Tap again to return to all messages.

### Category Filters
- Tap on any category pill (**Personal**, **Newsletters**, **Transactions**, or **Updates**) to view only messages belonging to that category. Tap the active chip again or tap **All** to clear the category filter.

## 3. Reading an Email

Tap any email in your inbox to open the Reader screen.

### Pure-Text Reader
- Emails are converted into clean, readable text.
- Heavy graphics, invisible tracking pixels, and annoying popups are completely stripped away.
- Web links are neatly presented with clean formatting so you can tap them safely.

### Sender & Date Details
- Below the email title, you'll see the sender name, their email address, and a friendly timestamp (e.g. *"Today 2:30 PM"* or *"Yesterday"*).
- **Expand Header Details**: Tap the timestamp or header area to reveal the full details, including full date, exact time, and recipient information. Tap again to collapse.

### View All Mails from this Sender
Want to review every email you've ever received from this person, company, or newsletter?
- **Top Bar Button**: Tap the **Inbox folder (All Inboxes)** icon in the top navigation bar at any time while reading.
- **Sender Details Card**: Tap the sender box to expand details, then tap the **"All Mails from Sender"** button.
- Both options instantly open the sender view showing every email received from that specific email address, along with unread counts and batch deletion options.

### One-Tap Unsubscribe
- If CleanMail detects that an email is a mailing list or newsletter, a clean **Unsubscribe** banner appears at the top.
- Tap **Unsubscribe** to open the official unsubscribe link directly.

### Lock or Delete from Reader
- **Lock / Unlock**: Tap the **Lock icon** in the top navigation bar to protect or unprotect this specific email.
- **Delete**: Tap the **Trash can icon** in the top navigation bar. A 5-second confirmation banner appears with an **Undo** option.

## 4. Sender Sweep (Inbox Decluttering)

CleanMail's **Sender Sweep** is the easiest way to purge noisy senders and promotional bulk mail.

### Navigating to Sender Sweep
- From the main Inbox screen, tap the **Broom (Cleaning Services)** icon in the top navigation bar.

### Reviewing Top Senders
- Senders are ranked by total message volume (e.g. *"Store Promo: 28 emails"*).
- Each card shows:
  - Sender initial avatar and name.
  - Email address and total email count.
  - The subject of their most recent email.

### Inspecting Senders & Viewing Emails
- **Tap the Sender's Name / Card**: Clicking directly on the sender title or card opens the **Sender Emails Screen**.
- Here you can browse every email sent by this specific sender, see which ones are unread, and swipe left to delete individual messages.

### Setting Sender Tags
- Tap the **Set Tag** button on any sender's card to quickly define how their future messages should be labeled and whether they should be auto-locked.

### Deleting All from a Sender
- Tap the red **Delete All** button on a sender card.
- A confirmation dialog appears showing how many emails will be removed.
- **Locked Emails are Protected**: Any emails from this sender that you locked will **not** be deleted.

## 5. Mailbox Management & Sync Progress

CleanMail supports managing multiple accounts with full visibility into how much of your mailbox is synced.

### Navigating to Mailboxes
- Tap the **Settings (gear)** icon in the top navigation bar $\rightarrow$ scroll down to **Mailboxes & Cloud** $\rightarrow$ tap **Manage Connected Mailboxes**.
- Alternatively, tap the **+ Add Mailbox** button inside the top filter bar.

### Sync Progress Bar (X of Y Synced)
Each connected mailbox card displays:
- **Account Identity**: Avatar with your chosen badge color, account name, email address, and provider badge.
- **Sync Progress Text**: Displays **`X of Y synced (Z%)`**, showing how many emails are cached locally out of the total available in your online mailbox.
- **Total in Mailbox**: Displays **`Total in mailbox: Y`** (reflecting the true message count in your Gmail account).
- **Visual Progress Bar**: A progress bar beneath the stats that fills up toward 100% (turning emerald green when completely synced).

### Smart Refresh & Batch Sync
Tap the **Sync (circular arrows)** icon on any mailbox card:
1. **Syncs Latest First**: It immediately checks for new incoming emails and updates your mailbox total.
2. **Batches Older Emails (100 at a time)**: If your latest emails are already caught up, it automatically searches for older un-synced emails and downloads them in **batches of 100**.
3. **Live Status**: Real-time progress updates appear under the progress bar (e.g. *"Syncing older emails: 40 of 100..."*).

### Connecting and Removing Mailboxes
- **Connect Gmail**: Tap **Connect Gmail Account** to sign in with Google Workspace or personal `@gmail.com`.
- **Disconnect Account**: Tap the trash icon next to any mailbox to disconnect it. This removes the account and clears cached emails from your device.

## 6. Settings & Customization

Tap the **Settings (gear)** icon in the top navigation bar to configure CleanMail.

### Themes
CleanMail includes multiple carefully crafted themes:
- **System Default**: Automatically mirrors your device's light or dark mode.
- **Paper Light**: Crisp, clean daylight theme for comfortable reading.
- **Focus Dark**: Low-glare dark palette designed for nighttime and OLED screens.
- **Terminal Nerd**: Retro hacker terminal theme featuring neon phosphor green typography, pitch-black OLED background, and monospace terminal styling throughout the entire application.

### Managing Sender Rules
- Under **Sender Preferred Tags & Rules**, view all active sender preferences.
- See which senders have custom categories or auto-lock enabled. Tap the delete icon to remove any rule.

### Cleaning Duplicate Emails
- Tap **Clean Up Duplicate Emails Across Mailboxes** to instantly scan and remove any duplicate cross-account entries.

### Activity & Diagnostic Logs
- Tap **View Activity & Diagnostic Logs** to view real-time audit logs of sync events, triage actions, and security status.
- You can search logs, filter by event type (Sync, Triage, Auth, System), and copy a formatted report with a single tap.
